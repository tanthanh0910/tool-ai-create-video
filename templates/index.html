• Tieng keu: Dat file .mp3 vao thu muc sounds/ (vd: lion.mp3, cat.mp3)

• Tim hinh anh/video THUC tu Pexels (mien phi)
• Doc ten thuc vat bang giong Viet (Edge TTS)
• Nhac nen: File sounds/plants/plants.mp3 (tu dong lap lai neu video dai hon)
• Khong co tieng keu, chi doc ten + nhac nen
⚠️ Can PEXELS_API_KEY trong file .env

🔀 Ghep Video + Audio
• Chon video va audio tu may tinh
• Video: MP4 | Audio: MP3/WAV/M4A
• Audio tu dong loop theo thoi luong video
• Neu nhap thoi luong: video se lap lai/cat
💡 De trong = lay thoi luong video lam goc, audio se tu dong loop lai neu ngan hon
💡 Chon it nhat 1-2 hieu ung de YouTube/TikTok khong danh dau "noi dung khong nguyen tac".
📌 Cac hieu ung se lam video khac ban goc, giup tranh bi canh bao.
🎞️ Ghep Nhieu Video
• Tai len nhieu video, moi video chon thoi luong rieng
• Cac video se duoc noi lai theo thu tu
• Audio se tu dong loop neu ngan hon tong thoi luong
